# PhotoFix Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 7, 2026  
**Developer contact:** lalitspatil1260@gmail.com

PhotoFix is an Android photo-editing app. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use its features.

## What PhotoFix Does

PhotoFix lets you select photos and prepare edited copies using tools such as compression, resizing, cropping, rotation, flipping, format conversion, ID photo sizing, and batch processing.

## Photos and On-Device Processing

When you select photos, PhotoFix reads the selected image files and processes their image data on your device. PhotoFix does not send selected photos to a PhotoFix-operated server. PhotoFix does not operate its own backend or photo-processing API.

Selected and processed image data may remain in app memory while you use the editing workflow. PhotoFix does not create a persistent PhotoFix photo library or account for selected photos. The Android image picker and operating system may use temporary resources while selecting media; their behavior can depend on the Android version and picker implementation.

## Saving to Your Gallery

If you choose **Save to gallery**, PhotoFix writes the processed image to your device's public Pictures/Gallery media collection. The saved copy remains on your device until you or another app remove it.

PhotoFix does not provide a separate in-app deletion or retention service for saved Gallery images. You can manage or delete those copies using your device's Gallery or file-management tools.

## Sharing

If you choose **Share**, PhotoFix passes the selected processed image to the Android sharing interface so you can choose another app or destination.

The current sharing implementation stages a share copy in PhotoFix's app-private cache and clears its share cache when a later share operation is prepared. Android or the receiving app may also create its own copy.

Sharing is initiated by you. After you send an image to another app or service, the recipient's privacy practices and handling of the file are outside PhotoFix's control. Review the recipient's policies before sharing.

## Advertising and Consent

PhotoFix integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent management.

Where applicable, UMP may display advertising consent choices or privacy options. PhotoFix requests ads only when its consent SDK indicates that ads may be requested. Ads may be unavailable because of consent choices, network availability, or ad-loading results. Photo-editing features do not depend on ad availability.

Google Mobile Ads and UMP are third-party services. Their SDKs and services may process information under Google's policies and according to applicable settings and consent choices. The information processed can depend on SDK versions, Android versions, Google configuration, and the final advertising setup.

For more information, review Google's privacy information:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

PhotoFix provides privacy options when the UMP SDK indicates that a privacy-options form is required. The availability and behavior of these options may depend on the applicable consent configuration.

## Accounts, Analytics, and Crash Reporting

PhotoFix does not require an account or login. The current app code does not implement a PhotoFix-owned analytics or crash-reporting service.

This statement does not cover diagnostics or information processing performed by third-party SDKs, the Android operating system, or other apps you choose to use. Those services may have their own data practices.

## Data Retention and Deletion

PhotoFix does not operate a backend for selected-photo processing and does not retain selected photos in a PhotoFix server account. Selected and processed image data are used during the active editing workflow.

When you save an image to your Gallery, a device-visible copy is created. You can remove it using your device's Gallery or file-management app.

For Android sharing, the current sharing implementation uses an app-private cache copy and clears its share cache when it prepares a subsequent share. The app does not configure a fixed retention period for this cache. The operating system manages cache storage, and a recipient may retain a shared copy independently.

Because PhotoFix does not maintain user accounts or a PhotoFix photo-processing backend, there is no account-based deletion request or server photo store available through the app.
